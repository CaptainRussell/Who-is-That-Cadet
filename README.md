# Who's that Cadet!

A name-and-face memorization game for **AFROTC Detachment 250, Iowa State University**. It helps cadets learn the 250th Cadet Wing and the Det 250 cadre.

**Play:** https://captainrussell.github.io/Who-is-That-Cadet/

## Drills
- **Study cards:** see the face, then flip the card to check yourself.
- **Name the face:** pick the right name from four choices.
- **Find the face:** pick the right photo for a name and rank.
- **Type the name:** type the last name; close spellings count.
- **Shadow mode:** the photo starts as a silhouette and is revealed when you answer.
- **Print flashcards:** double-sided sheets of any deck, 9 cards per page.

Build a deck by AS level, flight or cadre. Progress is saved in your browser only.

Cadet ranks, flights and wing positions are from the Fall 2026 250th Cadet Wing org chart and flight roster. Cadre information is from airforce.iastate.edu/cadre.

Created by Captain Scott Russell, Operations Flight Commander, AFROTC Detachment 250.
